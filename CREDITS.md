# Credits

The sky kingdom uses free CC0 3D models. Credit isn't required, but they deserve it.

| Pack | Creator | Used for | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| KayKit Adventurers 2.0 (free tier) | Kay Lousberg | Hero character and animations | https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-adventurers |
| Medieval Village MegaKit (Standard) | Quaternius | House walls, roofs and chimneys | https://quaternius.com/packs/medievalvillagemegakit.html |
| Nature Kit 2.1 | Kenney | Trees, bushes, flowers and rocks | https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit |

All three are licensed CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The compressed files in `public/models/` are built from these packs by `scripts/build-models.mjs`.
The source packs themselves are not stored in this repo.
